About Us

Aladham Company is a Gaza-based import, sourcing, and distribution company serving businesses throughout Palestine. We specialize in identifying, importing, and supplying products from international manufacturers and suppliers across multiple sectors, including food ingredients, food products, industrial chemicals, packaging materials, and general trading goods. Through our operations in Gaza and our partner network in the West Bank, we connect local businesses with reliable international sources while facilitating efficient procurement and distribution. Our mission is to build long-term partnerships with suppliers worldwide and provide Palestinian businesses with dependable access to high-quality products and materials.

Who We Serve

Manufacturers & Factories

We help manufacturers secure imported raw materials, food ingredients, industrial chemicals, packaging solutions, and production inputs from trusted international suppliers.

Retailers & Distributors

We supply retailers and distributors with high-quality imported products and dependable procurement solutions tailored to market needs.

Traders & Wholesalers

We work with local traders and wholesalers seeking reliable international sourcing channels and efficient supply solutions.

NGOs & International Organizations

Assisting international organizations operating in the Palestinian Territories by facilitating the sourcing and delivery of products and materials required for their projects and operations.

Global Supply Network

Aladham Company maintains sourcing relationships and supplier connections across Europe, Asia, the Middle East, and South America.

Our international network enables us to identify reliable manufacturers, exporters, and trading partners capable of supplying products that meet the requirements of Palestinian businesses and organizations.
