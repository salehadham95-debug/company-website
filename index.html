About Us

Aladham Company is a Gaza-based import, sourcing, and distribution company serving businesses throughout Palestine.


We specialize in identifying, importing, and supplying products from international manufacturers and suppliers across multiple sectors, including food ingredients, food products, industrial chemicals, packaging materials, and general trading goods.


Through our operations in Gaza and our partner network in the West Bank, we connect local businesses with reliable international sources while facilitating efficient procurement and distribution.


Our mission is to build long-term partnerships with suppliers worldwide and provide Palestinian businesses with dependable access to high-quality products and materials.

Who We Serve

Manufacturers & Factories

We assist manufacturers in securing imported food ingredients, industrial chemicals, packaging materials, and other production inputs required for manufacturing operations.

Retailers & Distributors

We provide retailers and distributors with access to imported food products, consumer goods, and supply solutions sourced from trusted international suppliers.

Traders & Wholesalers

We support traders and wholesalers by identifying international sourcing opportunities and facilitating procurement from manufacturers and exporters worldwide.

NGOs & International Organizations

We offer our services to humanitarian organizations, development agencies, and international institutions operating in the palestinian territories in sourcing products, materials, and equipment required for their programs and operations.
