About Us

Aladham Company is a Gaza-based import, sourcing, and distribution company serving businesses throughout Palestine. We specialize in identifying, importing, and supplying products from international manufacturers and suppliers across multiple sectors, including food ingredients, food products, industrial chemicals, packaging materials, and general trading goods. Through our operations in Gaza and our partner network in Hebron, West Bank, we connect local businesses with reliable international sources while facilitating efficient procurement and distribution. Our mission is to build long-term partnerships with suppliers worldwide and provide Palestinian businesses with dependable access to high-quality products and materials.

Activities

  • Import and distribution of food ingredients and food products
  • Industrial chemicals and raw materials
  • Packaging materials
  • General trading
  • Supplier sourcing and procurement
  • Wholesale distribution
