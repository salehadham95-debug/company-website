About Us

Aladham Company is a Gaza-based trading, import and distribution company serving businesses throughout Palestine.

We specialize in sourcing and supplying food ingredients, food products, industrial chemicals and raw materials, packaging materials and general trading goods.

Through our network in Gaza and our partners in Hebron, West Bank, we work to connect local businesses with reliable international suppliers and high-quality products.

Activities

  • Import and distribution of food ingredients and food products
  • Industrial chemicals and raw materials
  • Packaging materials
  • General trading
  • Supplier sourcing and procurement
  • Wholesale distribution
